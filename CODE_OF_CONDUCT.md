# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The goal of this project is a technical commons that is useful to everyone.
To that end, be constructive, be precise, and assume good faith. Disagreement
about design is welcome; making participation unpleasant is not.

Participants are expected to:

- **Critique work, not people.** "This gate would route incorrectly when X"
  is useful; "you clearly don't understand routing" is not.
- **Keep discussions technical.** Unsolicited commentary on personal
  characteristics, background, or identity is out of scope everywhere in
  this project's spaces.
- **Accept correction gracefully and correct gracefully.** Changing your
  mind on evidence is a strength.
- **Respect scope.** Maintainers decide what ships. Argue the decision once,
  with evidence; then let it rest or take it to a fresh issue with new
  information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, publishing
others' private information, sexualized language or imagery, sustained
disruption of discussions, and any other conduct a reasonable person would
consider hostile.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull
requests, discussions, and chat — and whenever an individual represents the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately by opening a
discussion marked for maintainers, or by contacting the repository owner
directly through GitHub. All reports are treated confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, mute, reject contributions, or ban participants whose
behavior violates this code. Decisions are recorded privately to the
enforcers and are final; repeated identical violations may be published in
redacted form for transparency.
